# Stitch Prompt — BTS Concert City Trip Planner (Mobile)

아래 프롬프트를 Stitch 웹(stitch.withgoogle.com)에 그대로 복사-붙여넣기 하세요.
Device Type: **MOBILE** 을 선택하세요.

---

## 📋 복사용 프롬프트 (Copy & Paste Below)

```
Design a premium mobile app screen for "NUBI" — a BTS Concert City Trip Planner.
The app helps K-pop fans build personalized travel itineraries around BTS concert cities worldwide.

CONCEPT: Instead of a boring form-based planner, users "shop" for travel experiences like adding items to a cart, then AI optimizes the route.

===== SCREEN LAYOUT (Top to Bottom) =====

【SECTION 1 — Header】
- App logo "NUBI" in top-left corner, small and elegant.
- Screen title: "나만의 방탄 투어" (My BTS Tour) in bold Korean typography.
- Subtitle: "Choose your travel archetype" in smaller text.

【SECTION 2 — BTS Member Archetype Carousel (Horizontal Scroll)】
- 7 elegant glassmorphism cards in a horizontal scrollable carousel.
- Each card represents a BTS member's travel personality archetype:
  • RM — "Culture Collector" (🎨 Art & Healing, Normal Pace)
  • V — "Romantic Wanderer" (💜 Romance & Art, Relaxed Pace)  
  • j-hope — "Aesthetic Explorer" (✨ Hotspot & Local, Packed Pace)
  • Jungkook — "Adrenaline Chaser" (⚡ Adventure & Food, Packed Pace)
  • Jimin — "Caring Companion" (🤝 Companion-focused, Normal Pace)
  • Jin — "Luxury Recharger" (👑 Premium & Relaxed, 3 spots/day max)
  • SUGA — "Ultimate Chiller" (😴 Minimal & Chill, Ultra-relaxed)
- Each card has: a stylized abstract avatar icon (not a real photo), the member name, archetype title, and 2-3 hashtag chips (e.g., #Adventure #Food).
- The currently selected card has a glowing purple (#8B5CF6) animated border.
- Below the carousel: a dynamic text area showing the selected archetype description.
  Example: "정국처럼 여행하기: 번지점프, 히든 맛집, 하루 8곳 풀코스!"

【SECTION 3 — Tinder-Style Swipe Card (Center, Main Area)】
- A large, prominent card takes up 60% of the screen.
- The card shows a recommended travel spot for the selected concert city (e.g., "LA Arts District").
- Card contents:
  • Full-bleed high-quality background photo of the place.
  • Bottom gradient overlay (dark) with:
    - Place name in bold white text.
    - Category chip (e.g., "🍽️ Restaurant" or "🎨 Art Spot").
    - Star rating (4.7 ⭐) and distance from concert venue ("1.2km from SoFi Stadium").
  • Swipe hint icons: ← "PASS" (gray) on left, → "ADD!" (purple glow) on right.
- The card should look slightly tilted (2-3 degrees) as if ready to be swiped.

【SECTION 4 — Floating Cart Bar (Bottom Sticky)】
- A sticky bottom bar with a unique design: instead of a plain shopping cart icon, it features a stylized "ARMY Bomb" (BTS lightstick) silhouette.
- The lightstick acts as a progress gauge: it fills up with glowing purple light as the user adds more spots.
- Text next to it: "2/5 슬롯 채움" (2/5 Slots Filled).
- A large call-to-action button on the right side:
  • When gauge is NOT full: Button is dimmed/disabled, text says "더 담아주세요" (Add more).
  • When gauge IS full: Button glows purple with text "🧠 AI 동선 최적화" (AI Route Optimization).
- Small helper text below: "Powered by Gemini — 현실 검증 포함" (Reality check included).

===== DESIGN SYSTEM =====

- Theme: Dark mode base (#0F0F14), with deep purple/violet accents (#8B5CF6, #6D28D9, #7C3AED).
- Glassmorphism: Translucent cards with backdrop-blur, subtle white border (rgba 255,255,255,0.1).
- Typography: Clean sans-serif (Inter or similar). Korean text should look premium.
- Border radius: 16px for cards, 24px for the main swipe card, 999px for chips.
- Shadows: Soft purple glow shadows on interactive elements.
- Overall feel: Premium, playful, K-pop fan culture, NOT corporate. Think "luxury concert merch app meets travel planner."
- Micro-interactions implied: The swipe card looks dynamic. The ARMY Bomb gauge looks like it could animate.

===== IMPORTANT NOTES =====
- This is a MOBILE screen (iPhone 15 Pro dimensions, 393×852).
- Use placeholder images for the place cards (stylish urban/travel photography).
- Korean and English text mixed naturally (bilingual UI).
- No real BTS member photos — use abstract/geometric avatar icons only.
- The design should make a K-pop fan instantly excited to use the app.
```
